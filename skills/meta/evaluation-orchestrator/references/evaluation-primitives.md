# Evaluation Primitives

Reusable operations for composing an evaluation workflow.

## reconstruct-target
Extract claims, rationale, alternatives, invariants, workflows, and unresolved questions with provenance.

## classify-epistemic-status
Separate observation, interpretation, assumption, hypothesis, decision, constraint, and unknown.

## recover-rationale
Reconstruct why a decision was made and what alternatives were rejected.

## derive-decision-boundary
State conditions supporting the current decision and conditions that would reverse it.

## steelman-current
Construct the strongest viable version of the current proposal and state its required conditions.

## steelman-rejected-alternative
Strengthen a rejected option before comparing it with the current choice.

## generate-counterexamples
Create verified, verification-needed, and synthetic cases that target specific claims or invariants.

## independent-reconstruction
Re-read the source without seeded concerns or upstream summaries to detect framing omissions.

## test-implementation-ambiguity
Ask whether two competent practitioners can produce materially different implementations from the same instructions.

## test-operational-enforceability
Map each rule to prevention, detection, repair, and ownership mechanisms.

## design-structural-alternative
Generate an alternative with different boundaries, decomposition, authority, or state ownership.

## compare-alternatives
Compare options on explicit decision dimensions and repair costs, not superficial pros/cons.

## calibrate-severity
Tie severity to consequence, likelihood, detectability, reversibility, and blast radius.

## synthesize-with-dissent
Produce a decision while preserving unresolved conflicts, confidence, and needed evidence.

## convert-critique-to-action
Attach an alternative, distinguishing test, repair step, or escalation question to every material criticism.

## localize-evaluation-failure
Find the earliest stage responsible for a weak or misleading evaluation.

## promote-evaluation-learning
Turn repeated correction deltas into tested, versioned evaluation improvements.

## compile-decision-record
Bind observed design, rationale, invariants, attack, defense, verification, and alternatives into one traceable decision unit.

## verify-primary-source-supremacy
Compare normalized IR against primary sources and report omissions or contradictions before relying on upstream framing.

## derive-case-generation-axes
Decompose the target into state, transition, ownership, lifecycle, authority, representation, environment, and recovery dimensions before generating cases.

## replay-regression-suite
Run golden, boundary, prior-failure, and holdout cases and compare quality, cost, context growth, and human intervention.
