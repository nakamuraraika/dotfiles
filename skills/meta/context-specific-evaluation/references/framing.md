# Evaluation Framing

## Evaluation modes

- Review: identify strengths, defects, and improvements in one target.
- Audit: verify compliance with explicit obligations or controls.
- Comparison: explain meaningful differences between alternatives.
- Selection: recommend an option and specify reversal conditions.
- Critique: test reasoning, assumptions, and implications.
- Quality gate: decide whether progression is acceptable.

## Frame template

1. What exactly is being evaluated and which version?
2. What decision will the evaluation support?
3. Who bears the consequences of that decision?
4. What outcomes define success?
5. Which constraints are non-negotiable?
6. What evidence is authoritative, contextual, anecdotal, or absent?
7. Which concerns are intentionally outside scope?
8. What would materially reverse the verdict?

## High-information questions

Ask a question early only when its answer could:

- reverse the verdict;
- change a critical criterion;
- invalidate a central assumption;
- distinguish between two costly alternatives;
- reveal a hidden mandatory constraint.

Otherwise, proceed with a labeled hypothesis.
